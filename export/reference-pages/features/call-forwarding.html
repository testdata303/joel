Call forwarding

Call forwarding sends calls to your business number to the right person's mobile: one phone, several ringing at once, or different destinations by schedule. Customers dial one number; your team answers on the phones they already carry, and callbacks come from the business number.

Different businesses, different rules.

Small team

One number, everyone's mobile

The whole team answers the business line on their own phones, several cell phones ringing at once, first pickup wins, personal numbers never shown.

Trades

Emergencies to whoever is on call

A plumbing company routes emergency calls to the on-call plumber's mobile and swaps the destination when the rotation changes, the number on the truck stays the same.

Solo + growing

Founder's mobile now, teammates later

A startup forwards everything to the founder's mobile on day one. The first hire is a new destination, not a new phone system.

After hours

Evenings to an answering provider

After closing, forward to your own answering provider or the third-party AI answering service you choose. Numberline just routes the call.

What people ask about call forwarding

What does the caller see when a call is forwarded?
Nothing changes for the caller, they dialed the business number and they're talking to your business. Forwarding is invisible from their side.
What shows on my phone when a forwarded call comes in?
The call arrives with business caller ID, so you know it's a business call before you answer, not a personal one.
What happens if nobody answers a forwarded call?
The call goes to business voicemail, and the team gets the recording and transcript in the shared inbox. The message never lands in anyone's personal voicemail.
Can I forward to a landline or an existing business phone?
Yes. Destinations can be any phone, a teammate's mobile, the existing business or practice phone, or an outside answering provider you choose.
Can forwarding change by time of day?
Yes. Business hours can ring the team while evenings and weekends ring only the on-call mobile, or forward to your own answering provider or the third-party AI answering service you choose.
Does forwarding cost more per person?
No. Numberline has unlimited users instead of per-seat pricing, so forwarding to another teammate's mobile never changes the price. See pricing.
