What is a business phone number?

A business phone number is the number customers call and text to reach your company. With Numberline it is more than a line: it carries a menu and extensions, routes to the right person, keeps texts in a shared inbox, and turns voicemail into transcripts, so communication belongs to the business.

Why not just use your cell?

Customers trust a business number more than a personal cell, and a business number keeps your private line out of circulation. More important, it lets the whole team share the work: calls route to the right person, texts land in a shared inbox, and voicemail becomes a transcript. The number, and the customer relationship, belong to the business.

Questions about business phone numbers

What is a business phone number?
It is the number customers call and text to reach your company. With Numberline it also carries routing, extensions, shared texts, and voicemail transcripts, so it belongs to the business, not one person.
Can I get a local or toll-free number?
Yes. Choose a local number in the area code you want or a toll-free number, and use both together if it helps. See local and toll-free numbers.
Can I keep my current number?

Can I have more than one number?
Yes. Start with the business line you need now and add numbers as needed, for a second location, a department, or a campaign.
Can the team share calls and texts?
Yes. Calls route to the right person, texts land in a shared inbox, and voicemail becomes a transcript, so communication stays with the business.
Can I text from my business number?
Yes. Customers can text your business number, and the conversation lands in a shared inbox for the team to answer.

No. Numberline forwards calls to the cell phone you already have. There is nothing to buy or install. Compatible desk phones are optional, never required.
