Use case

A business phone that rings multiple cell phones.

A business number can ring several cell phones at the same time. Calls to your Numberline number ring every mobile you add, yours, your assistant's, the on-call tech's, and whoever answers first takes the call. Everyone else's phone stops ringing, and texts and voicemails still land in the shared inbox.

Here’s how you set it up.

Step 1

Get your business number

Choose a local or toll-free business number, or bring the one your customers already call. This is the number on your website and invoices.

Step 2

Add the mobiles that should ring

Add each teammate and point call forwarding at their own mobiles. Ring everyone at once, or one after another, your choice per menu option.

Step 3

Texts and voicemail stay shared

Whoever answers, texts and voicemails to the business number land in the shared inbox, and callbacks come from the business number via the mobile app.

Nothing to install to receive a call. Calls forward to the phones your team already carries, on any carrier.

Different ways to set this up.

In order

Ring one phone, then the next

Instead of ringing everyone at once, ring the owner's mobile first, then the assistant's after a few seconds. Good when one person should usually answer and the rest are backup.

By menu option

Different calls, different phones

Each menu option rings its own set of mobiles: new business rings two phones, existing customers ring the person who knows them. Regulars can dial an extension directly.

After hours

Evenings and weekends

After closing, ring only the on-call mobile, or forward to your own answering provider or the third-party AI answering service you choose. Numberline just routes the call.

What people ask about ringing multiple phones

What happens if two people answer at the same time?
The first person to pick up takes the call, and everyone else's phone stops ringing. There is no conference or crosstalk, the caller only ever talks to one person.
Does my personal number show when I answer or call back?
No. Customers only ever see the business number. When you call back through the Numberline mobile app, the call comes from the business number, so your personal cell stays private. See personal cell vs business phone system.
How many cell phones can ring at once?
Add everyone who answers for the business, Numberline has unlimited users instead of per-seat pricing, so adding another teammate's mobile never changes the price.
Do the phones need to be the same carrier or type?
No. Calls forward to any mobile, iPhone or Android, any carrier. There is nothing to install to receive a forwarded call.
What happens if nobody answers?
The call goes to business voicemail. The team gets the recording and a transcript in the shared inbox, and whoever is free calls back from the business number.
Can different menu options ring different groups of phones?
Yes. Each menu option or extension has its own forwarding, so sales can ring two mobiles while support rings a different one. See call forwarding.
