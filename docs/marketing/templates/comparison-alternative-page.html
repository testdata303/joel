Questions about switching from Google Voice

Is Google Voice bad?
No. Google Voice is a solid free second number for one person. The difference for a business is the model: it charges per user, ties access to a Google account, and does not connect to tools like Slack, WhatsApp, or a third-party AI answering service.
Can I move my number from Google Voice?
Numberline is designed to support bringing your current business number. Join early access and we'll share porting details when setup opens.
What does Numberline add?
The same phone-system essentials, without the Google tie: unlimited users with no per-user pricing, a shared SMS and WhatsApp inbox, voicemail transcripts, Slack notifications, webhooks, and forwarding to third-party AI answering services.
Do you charge per user?
No. Users are unlimited. Add teammates, assistants, and contractors without another seat fee.
Can more than one person answer?
Yes. Route calls to different people, share a text inbox, and give everyone who needs it visibility in Slack.
Is it a phone system or a second number?
A phone system: greetings, extensions, call forwarding, shared texts, and voicemail transcripts on one business number. See how Numberline works.
Do I need new phones or equipment?
No. Numberline forwards calls to the cell phone you already have. There is nothing to buy or install. Compatible desk phones are optional, never required.
