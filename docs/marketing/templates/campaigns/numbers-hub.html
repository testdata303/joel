The number is where the business starts.

Every Numberline setup begins with a number: a local number in the area code your customers know, a toll-free number that reads national, or the business phone number you already have. Whichever you choose, it arrives with the whole phone system around it: forwarding, shared texts, voicemail transcripts, and callbacks from the business line.
