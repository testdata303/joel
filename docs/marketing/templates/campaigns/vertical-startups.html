Questions founders ask

Do I need a business phone before I have customers?
You need the number before you need the phone system. The number goes on your website, filings, and profiles you're setting up right now, and changing it later means updating everything. Answering it stays as simple as your mobile ringing.
Can I just use my cell number for my startup?
You can, but the number gets published everywhere and follows you for good, every list, database, and customer keeps it. A business number gives customers something permanent while your personal cell stays private. See personal cell vs business phone system.
What does day one actually look like?
A local or toll-free number, forwarded to your own mobile, with business voicemail and transcripts, shared texting, and callbacks from the business number. That's the whole setup until you want more.
What happens when I hire my first person?
Add them and forward calls to their mobile too. Numberline has unlimited users instead of per-seat pricing, so a hire is a new destination, not a new bill.
Do I need to know about extensions and greetings now?
No. They exist when you want them, a greeting when you want to sound more established, extensions when there are enough people to route between. Nothing about the day-one setup has to be planned around them.
Can investors and customers text the number?
Yes. Texts to the business number land in a shared inbox, and you reply from the business number, from the mobile app, wherever you are.
