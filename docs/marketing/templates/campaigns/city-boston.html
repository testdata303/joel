Boston number questions

Do I need to be in Boston to get a Boston number?
No. A Boston number is a signal to Boston customers, not a residency requirement. Calls forward to your team's mobiles wherever they are.
Is 857 worse than 617?
No, just newer. Both cover the same territory, and Bostonians dial both every day. 617 carries more history; 857 has better availability.
