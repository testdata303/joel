## 0. The governing principle (owner, 2026-07-28, adopted)

**The product catalog and the URL architecture are different artifacts.**
- The **/features hub is the product catalog**: every real capability appears there, permanently, whether or not it has a page.
- The **standalone pages are the knowledge graph**: only capabilities that carry a distinct buying question get URLs.
- The two are joined by ANCHORS, never forced 1:1.

Challenged and refined, twice:
1. **Every catalog entry still has exactly one canonical home** - a page OR a named section anchor on its owner page (e.g. `/features/call-forwarding#schedules`). A capability chip that links nowhere is an orphan pattern and forbidden. This preserves the no-orphan rule and makes promotion mechanical (anchor becomes page; anchor keeps working).
2. **The catalog is gated by product truth.** Pre-launch law: never promise what the product can't show. Capabilities enter the catalog when they are real (or brand-paragraph-promised: WhatsApp, webhooks), never for grid symmetry. No invented capabilities (e.g. call recording is NOT in the catalog until the owner confirms it exists).
3. **One capability, one canonical truth (owner, 2026-07-28, permanent).** The Features catalog (tools/features.json) owns every capability's definition: its name, buying question, owner, and status. Every other surface - homepage, features hub, footer, pricing, comparison tables, future documentation and onboarding - REFERENCES the catalog; none copies, re-types, or keeps a separate list. A capability wording change is a registry change plus a sweep, never an edit to one surface.

## 3. Promotion criteria (section -> page)

A capability graduates ONLY when ALL hold:
1. **Distinct question:** its buying question is not answerable by the owner page's H1 answer (not a restatement).
2. **Demand:** repeated direct asks in the capability's own words (search console, sales/support, AI-assistant citations) sustained over >=1 quarter. No speculative pages.
3. **Depth:** the section honestly needs >=4-5 substantial blocks (own FAQ set, own sample setup) and visibly crowds its owner.
4. **Link gravity:** >=3 pages want to link the capability specifically, with anchor text that doesn't fit the owner page.
Mechanics on promotion: new page 301-safe (owner#anchor keeps working via a stub link or redirect), catalog row re-pointed, hub rosters + footer reviewed under the capacity rule, graph updated - one commit.
Demotion mirrors it: a page that can no longer defend 1-4 merges back into its owner with a 301 (precedent: the July consolidations).

### 9d. Canonical feature registry - ADOPTED

Challenged: over-engineering for a static site? No - the site already runs on this pattern (comparison-table specimen as DATA SOURCE, tools/site-pages.json as page manifest), and five surfaces (homepage function list, /features hub, footer, /pricing, future comparison tables) currently repeat feature names by hand. Rendering stays plain HTML (no build step); the registry is the VERIFIED source of truth: `node tools/site-system.mjs verify` checks every surface against it.

**tools/features.json schema (one entry per catalog row):**
`id` (slug, stable forever) · `name` (display, verbatim where label law applies) · `category` (numbers | extensions | routing | voicemail | texting | connections - internal id; display names per category live in a `categories` block, e.g. connections -> "Apps & AI") · `buying_question` · `owner_page` · `anchor` (null when standalone) · `standalone` (bool) · `status` (live | promised | planned - promised = brand-paragraph capabilities pre-launch; planned rows NEVER render) · `surfaces` (array of surface ids this capability may appear on: `["homepage","footer","pricing","comparison", ...]` - REPLACES the v3-draft per-surface booleans after the owner's future-proofing challenge: adding a surface (docs, onboarding, sales one-pager) is a new string, never a schema change) · `promotion_trigger` (C-entries only) · `notes`.
Verifier rules: every rendered catalog row exists in the registry with a matching link; each surface renders only capabilities whose `surfaces` include that surface's id; every non-standalone entry's owner_page#anchor resolves; no `planned` entry renders anywhere; category display names come from the `categories` block only.

## 10. Five-year fitness check

- User understanding: catalog = one scannable matrix; pages = one buying question each.
- Buying intent: every A-page opens with its question; hub rows land on answers, not marketing.
- AI retrieval: anchors give assistants stable citation targets for section-level facts; pillar definitions stay verbatim and singular.
- Search: no thin pages, no keyword skins; promotion criteria prevent both bloat and starvation.
- Scalability: growth happens in data (catalog rows, cities, verticals, partners), each gated; the URL set grows slower than the product.
- Product breadth: expressed at the hub and footer without a single thin page.
