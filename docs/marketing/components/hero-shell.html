Use case

A business phone that rings multiple cell phones.

A business number can ring several cell phones at the same time. Calls to your Numberline number ring every mobile you add, yours, your assistant's, the on-call tech's, and whoever answers first takes the call. Everyone else's phone stops ringing, and texts and voicemails still land in the shared inbox.
