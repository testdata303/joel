In order

Ring one phone, then the next

Instead of ringing everyone at once, ring the owner's mobile first, then the assistant's after a few seconds. Good when one person should usually answer and the rest are backup.

By menu option

Different calls, different phones

Each menu option rings its own set of mobiles: new business rings two phones, existing customers ring the person who knows them. Regulars can dial an extension directly.

After hours

Evenings and weekends

After closing, ring only the on-call mobile, or forward to your own answering provider or the third-party AI answering service you choose. Numberline just routes the call.
