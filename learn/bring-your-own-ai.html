How Numberline works

Numberline is your phone system. The AI answering service you choose is a destination.

Two big benefits:

Benefit 1

Never get stuck with one AI answering service

New AI answering services appear all the time, and the best fit for your business can change. If your phone number lives inside one of them, leaving means moving your whole phone system. With Numberline your number stays put. You forward calls to the third-party AI answering service you choose, and you can point them somewhere else later.

  • Your business number stays with Numberline
  • Use the AI answering service you choose, like Vapi or Bland
  • Change destinations later if your needs change
Your phone system is not tied to one provider.
Benefit 2

Send each extension wherever you want

You decide where every extension goes, and they don't all have to go to the same place. Your support line can go to a third-party AI answering service, your personal extension can ring your own cell phone, and an after-hours line can go to a live answering service. Different extensions, different destinations.

  • Support line → a third-party AI answering service
  • Personal extension → rings your own cell phone
  • After-hours line → a live answering service
Every extension goes exactly where it makes sense.
Why this matters

Say you want to try an AI answering service. Sign up with it directly and it often becomes your phone company too: it hands you a new number, or you give it yours. The day you want something different, switching means moving your number and setting everything up again.

Use it where it fits

You pick which lines go to an AI answering service and which don't. A lot of businesses want a real person on the phone during the day, and only use an AI answering service for after hours, weekends, or when everyone's on the other line.

Questions

Third-party AI answering services, common questions

What does "third-party AI answering service" mean?
An AI answering service made by another company that answers calls for your business. Numberline does not force one on you. You choose the service that fits, forward an extension to it, and Numberline stays your phone number and routing. The destination can change whenever you like.
Does Numberline answer calls with AI?
No. Numberline is not an AI receptionist. Numberline routes calls where you decide, and voicemails come with transcripts and summaries (English for now) so you can read what callers needed. If you want AI answering, you connect the third-party service you choose.
Should my whole business move to an AI answering service?
For most businesses, no. A person is still the best experience for most daytime calls. AI is most valuable in the gaps: after hours, weekends, overflow, or a single line. Numberline lets you apply AI exactly where it helps and keep the rest human.
What if I don't like the AI answering service I picked?
Change it. Because the AI is a routing destination and not your platform, you can switch to a different service, narrow when it runs, or point the extension back to a person. Your number and setup stay where they are.
Which AI answering services can I connect?
Third-party AI answering services like Vapi and Bland, or your own voice agent. Numberline is designed to work with services that can take a forwarded call. Join early access and we'll share connection details when setup opens.
Why is that better than an AI phone product with its own number?
When the AI and your phone number are the same product, switching means moving your entire phone system. With Numberline, the AI answering service is a destination. If it stops fitting, you change the destination and your number stays put.
