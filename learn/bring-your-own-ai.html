How Numberline works

Numberline is your phone system. Forward calls to any AI answering service you want.

Two big benefits:

Benefit 1

Never get stuck with one AI answering service

New AI answering services pop up every week, and the best one keeps changing. If your phone number lives inside one of them, leaving means moving your whole phone system and your number too. With Numberline your number stays put. You just forward a call to whichever AI answering service you want - and pick a different one the day you find a better one.

  • Your number stays with Numberline, always
  • Try any AI answering service - Vapi, Bland, others
  • Switch to a different one anytime, in seconds
You're never locked into one company.
Benefit 2

Send each extension wherever you want

You decide where every extension goes - and they don't all have to go to the same place. Your sales line can go to one AI answering service, your support line to another, your personal extension can just ring your own cell phone, and an after-hours line can go to a live answering service. Different extensions, different destinations - your choice.

  • Sales line → an AI built for sales calls
  • Personal extension → rings your own cell phone
  • After-hours line → a live answering service
Every extension goes exactly where it makes sense.

Questions

Bring your own AI - common questions

What does "bring your own AI" mean?
It means Numberline doesn't force one AI answering service on you. You choose the AI answering service that fits your business - Vapi, Bland, or your own - and forward any extension to it. Numberline stays your phone number and routing layer; the AI is a destination you can change whenever you like.
Does Numberline include any AI on its own?
Yes. Voicemail transcripts, AI call and voicemail summaries, and AI-generated greeting voices are included on every plan. This built-in AI helps you understand and manage calls - it never answers the phone for you or decides how a call is routed.
Should my whole business move to an AI answering service?
For most businesses, no. A person is still the best experience for most daytime calls. AI is most valuable in the gaps - after hours, weekends, overflow, or a single line. Numberline lets you apply AI exactly where it helps and keep the rest human.
What if I don't like the AI answering service I picked?
Change it. Because the AI is a routing destination and not your platform, you can switch to a different service, narrow when it runs, or point the extension back to a person in seconds. Your number and setup stay exactly where they are.
Which AI answering services can I connect?
Any service you can reach over SIP, including Vapi and Bland AI, or your own custom voice agent. You connect it once and route the extensions you choose to it.
Why is that better than an all-in-one AI phone product?
In an all-in-one product, the AI and your phone number are the same thing. If the AI gets worse, more expensive, or isn't right for you, switching means re-platforming your entire phone system. With bring-your-own AI, the AI is swappable and your number stays put - so you're never locked into one vendor's roadmap.
