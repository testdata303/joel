Why bring your own

The risk of an all-in-one AI phone service

When your number, routing, and AI are the same product, the AI is not a feature you use - it is a vendor you are married to. If it gets worse, gets expensive, or is simply wrong for your business, your whole phone system is what you have to move.

Use it where it fits

Most calls don't need AI. That's exactly why routing beats replacing.

For most small businesses, a real person answering is still the best experience during the day. AI shines in the gaps - after hours, overflow when everyone's busy, or one specific line - not as a wholesale replacement for your team.

Take a dental office. Going 100% AI answering would be a mistake - patients calling about pain want a person. But an AI agent for after-hours and weekends, capturing new-patient requests and emergencies, is a clear win. With AnyPhone you set that up on one extension, keep the rest human, and dial it back the day it isn't pulling its weight.

Questions

Bring your own AI - common questions

What does "bring your own AI" mean?
It means AnyPhone doesn't force one AI answering service on you. You choose the AI voice agent that fits your business - Vapi, Bland, or your own - and forward any extension to it. AnyPhone stays your phone number and routing layer; the AI is a destination you can change whenever you like.
Does AnyPhone include any AI on its own?
Yes. Voicemail transcripts, AI call and voicemail summaries, and AI-generated greeting voices are included on every plan. This built-in AI helps you understand and manage calls - it never answers the phone for you or decides how a call is routed.
Should my whole business move to an AI answering service?
For most businesses, no. A person is still the best experience for most daytime calls. AI is most valuable in the gaps - after hours, weekends, overflow, or a single line. AnyPhone lets you apply AI exactly where it helps and keep the rest human.
What if I don't like the AI service I picked?
Change it. Because the AI is a routing destination and not your platform, you can switch to a different service, narrow when it runs, or point the extension back to a person in seconds. Your number and setup stay exactly where they are.
Which AI answering services can I connect?
Any service you can reach over SIP, including Vapi and Bland AI, or your own custom voice agent. You connect it once and route the extensions you choose to it.
Why is that better than an all-in-one AI phone product?
In an all-in-one product, the AI and your phone number are the same thing. If the AI gets worse, more expensive, or isn't right for you, switching means re-platforming your entire phone system. With bring-your-own AI, the AI is swappable and your number stays put - so you're never locked into one vendor's roadmap.
