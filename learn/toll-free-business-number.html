Why it matters

Useful for a national or broader presence.

A toll-free number can be useful when you want a broader or more national presence: businesses serving customers across several states, consultants and agencies with national clients, or ecommerce and service businesses that don't want to look tied to one city. It is not automatically more professional, and a local number can feel more familiar nearby, so many small businesses use both. Numberline lets you pick what fits and back it with a real phone system.

Questions

Questions about toll-free business numbers

What is a toll-free number?
A toll-free number uses a prefix like 800, 888, or 877 and is not tied to one city. It reads as national and can make a business look established across regions.
Is toll-free better than local?
Not always. Toll-free fits a broader presence, while a local number can feel more familiar nearby. Many businesses use both.
Can I use toll-free and local together?
Yes. Start with a toll-free number and add a local number, or add numbers as needed.
Can I pick the prefix?
Yes. Choose from 800, 888, 877, and more, or a memorable custom number.
Can I text from a toll-free number?
Yes, after the required SMS compliance approval. Texts land in a shared inbox for the team to answer.
Is it just a number, or a phone system?
Both. Your toll-free number comes with routing, extensions, shared texts, and voicemail transcripts. See virtual phone system.
Do I need new phones or equipment?
No. Numberline forwards calls to the cell phone you already have. There is nothing to buy or install. Compatible desk phones are optional, never required.
Can I keep my existing number?
Numberline is designed to support bringing your current business number. Join early access and we'll share porting details when setup opens.
