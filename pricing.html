Questions, answered.

Do you charge per user?
No. Users are unlimited on every plan. Plan limits apply to numbers, extensions, outbound minutes, and advanced capabilities.
Are extensions unlimited?
Lite includes 2 extensions. Grow includes 5. Max includes unlimited extensions.
What counts toward my minutes?
Only outbound minutes count. Inbound calls don't use your included minutes.
What happens if I exceed my minutes?
You can add more outbound minutes whenever you need them. Pricing is shared at launch.
Can I keep my current number?
Numberline is designed to support bringing your current business number. Join early access and we'll share porting details when setup opens.
Can I text from Lite?
Yes, after the required SMS compliance approval.
Do I need hardware?
No. Numberline works on the mobile phone and computer you already have. Compatible SIP desk phones are optional on Grow and Max.
Is support included?
Yes. Human support is included with every plan.
