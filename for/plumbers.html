What you get

Everything a plumbing business needs from a phone.

Business number

Get a new number for your service area or port the one your customers already have. It becomes the single number for the whole business.

ExamplePut one number on the truck, the yard sign, and the invoices instead of your personal cell.

Simple call routing

Set up a short menu and point each option at the right person. Callers choose what they need and the call goes straight there.

ExamplePress 1 for new jobs, press 3 for an emergency, press 0 for the owner.

Shared text messages

Texts to the business number land in one shared inbox. Anyone on the team can read and reply, so messages don't get stuck on one phone.

ExampleA customer texts a photo of a leak and whoever is free replies from the shared inbox.

Voicemail and transcripts

When nobody answers, the call goes to voicemail. You get the message as a transcript and an email so you can read it and call back.

ExampleRead the voicemail transcript between jobs instead of stopping to listen to it.

Missed-call and voicemail alerts

When nobody answers, Numberline can send a missed-call alert and voicemail transcript so the owner, office, or on-call person knows to follow up. If your team uses Slack, Numberline can send notifications there too.

ExampleA missed emergency call sends an alert and the on-call plumber calls the customer back fast.

Overflow or after-hours answering

Route overflow or after-hours calls to a third-party answering service you choose, and change it when you need to. Connect it, switch providers, or turn it off without changing your number.

Questions

Questions plumbers ask

Can I use my existing plumbing business number?
Numberline is designed to support bringing your current business number. Join early access and we'll share porting details when setup opens.
Can calls ring my cell phone?
Yes. You can forward calls to your cell, an admin's mobile, an existing business phone, or the outside answering provider you choose. Your personal number stays private because customers only ever see the business number.
Can I send and receive texts from the business number?
Yes. Texts to the business number land in one shared inbox. The team can read and reply from the same place, so a customer message never gets stuck on one person's phone.
Can different types of calls go to different people?
Yes. Set up a simple menu and route each option however you like. New jobs can ring the owner and office, emergencies can ring the owner and a backup plumber, and existing customers can ring the dispatcher.
What happens if nobody answers?
The call goes to voicemail. You get a transcript and an email, and a missed-call alert helps the owner, office, or on-call person follow up. If your team uses Slack, Numberline can send notifications there too.
Is Numberline dispatch software?
No. Numberline is your phone system. It helps calls and texts reach the right person. It does not schedule jobs, assign technicians, or manage invoices.
Do we need new phones or equipment?
No. Calls forward to the cell phones your crew already carry. There is nothing to buy or install. Compatible desk phones are optional later, never required.
What happens when we add another plumber?
Add an extension, invite them, and route emergencies or new jobs to their cell. The number your customers call never changes.
Is there a contract?
