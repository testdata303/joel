Your front desk, providers, and owner shouldn't all become separate phone systems.

Med spa communication often starts from different places: Google, Instagram, referrals, ads, the website, and existing clients. Then it gets answered through a front desk phone, an owner's cell, a provider's cell, and scattered texts. The result is not only missed calls. It is disorganized follow-up, personal numbers getting handed out, and client conversations living with the wrong person.

  • New clients call while the front desk is with someone in the office
  • Providers get pulled into client texts on personal phones
  • The owner becomes the backup receptionist
  • After-hours inquiries disappear until the next day
  • Adding another provider or location creates more phone chaos
What Numberline is

The phone layer for your med spa.

Numberline is not medspa software. It is the phone system that sits between your clients and the people who can help them. Get a local or toll-free business line, forward calls to the right person, manage shared texts and voicemails, send transcripts to email, and add extensions or answering services as the practice grows.

Your booking, charting, payments, memberships, forms, and client records stay in the medspa software you already use.

Questions

Questions med spa founders ask

Is Numberline medspa software?
No. Numberline is a phone system. It does not replace your booking, charting, payments, forms, memberships, POS, or practice management software.
Can I use Numberline with my current medspa software?
Yes. Numberline is designed to sit beside the software you already use. Your medspa software can keep managing bookings, records, and operations while Numberline handles the business line, call forwarding, shared texts, voicemails, and transcripts.
Can calls ring several phones at once?
Yes. Numberline can forward calls to your mobile, a manager's, provider's, or assistant's mobile, the practice phone, or an outside answering provider.
Can I keep my personal cell number private?
Yes. Numberline helps your med spa use a business line for calls and callbacks instead of giving every client the founder's or provider's personal cell number.
Can I bring my current med spa number?
Numberline is designed to support bringing your current business number. Join early access and we'll share porting details when setup opens.
Can I add numbers for multiple locations?
Numberline is designed so businesses can add numbers as needed. That can include different lines for locations, brands, or business needs.
Does Numberline provide AI answering?
Numberline is not an AI receptionist. It is designed to let your phone system forward calls to the people, services, or third-party AI answering service you choose when your business needs that.
