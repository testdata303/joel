Real estate leads do not wait.

A buyer who calls about a listing is calling three agents. When you are at a showing, on another call, or with a client, that call goes to voicemail and the buyer keeps dialing.

If nobody answers, they reach the next agent. And when every call runs through your personal cell, the number gets passed around, the whole team cannot help, and the relationship lives on one phone instead of with your business.

What you get

Business number

Get a new local number or bring the one your clients already have. It becomes the number on your signs, listings, and cards instead of your personal cell.

ExamplePut one number on the yard sign and the listing so buyers reach your business, not your private line.

Simple call routing

Set up a short menu and point each option at the right person. Buyers, sellers, and current clients can reach different people without playing phone tag.

ExamplePress 1 for buyers, press 3 for a current transaction, press 0 for the office.

Shared text messages

Texts to the business number land in one shared inbox. You and your assistant can both read and reply, so a buyer's message never gets stuck on one phone.

ExampleA buyer texts about a showing time and your assistant replies from the shared inbox while you are out.

Voicemail and transcripts

When you cannot pick up, the call goes to voicemail. You get the message as a transcript and an email so you can read it between showings and call back.

ExampleRead a seller's voicemail transcript on your way to the next appointment instead of stopping to listen.

Slack notifications

Numberline posts missed calls, new texts, and voicemails to Slack so you or your assistant can follow up before the lead calls someone else.

ExampleA missed call from a new buyer shows up in Slack and your assistant calls back within minutes.

Connect the AI answering service you choose

Route any menu option or extension to a third-party AI answering service, including real estate answering tools, not just after hours. Connect it, switch providers, or turn it off any time without changing your number.

Questions

Questions real estate agents ask

Can I keep my calls off my personal cell?
Yes. Clients call and text your Numberline business number, and you can forward calls to your cell so your personal number stays private. You can put the business number on signs, listings, and cards.
Can I bring my current number?
Yes, and porting is free. You get a temporary number instantly, so you can set up and take calls while the transfer completes. Nothing goes dark. When the port finishes, you can keep the temporary number free as a second line (one per business).
Can my assistant see and reply to texts?
Yes. Texts to the business number land in one shared inbox, so your assistant or a teammate can read and reply without the message getting stuck on your phone.
Can new leads and current clients ring different people?
Yes. Set up a simple menu and route each option however you like. New buyers can ring you and your assistant, while a current transaction can ring a coordinator or teammate.
What happens to missed calls?
The call goes to voicemail. You get a transcript and an email, and the missed call posts to Slack so you or your assistant can call the lead back quickly.
Is Numberline a CRM?
No. Numberline is your phone system. It helps calls and texts reach the right person. It does not manage your pipeline, your listings, or route leads from third-party sites. It works alongside the tools you already use.
Do I need new phones or equipment?
No. Calls forward to the cell phones you and your assistant already carry. There is nothing to buy or install. Compatible desk phones are optional later, never required.
Is there a contract?
No. Numberline is month-to-month. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime.
