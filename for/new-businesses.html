The first number you use follows you forever.

When you start a business, your number ends up everywhere fast: your website, your Google Business Profile, invoices, business cards, the sign on the door. If that number is your personal cell, it's out there for good.

Strangers calling at dinner. Spam that never stops. No way to tell a business call from a personal one. And when you eventually hire, there's no way to hand off "your" number. Starting with a real business number costs a few minutes now and saves all of that later.

Questions

Starting-a-business questions, answered.

Do I need to buy a phone or any equipment?
No. AnyPhone forwards business calls to the cell phone you already have. There's nothing to buy, install, or plug in. Desk phones are supported later if you ever want them; they're never required.
Will callers see my personal cell number?
No. Callers dial and see your business number. Your personal number stays private. It never appears on caller ID, your website, or anywhere else.
I already have a number on my flyers. Can I keep it?
Yes. You can bring an existing business number to AnyPhone. You get a temporary number right away so you can set everything up and take calls while the move completes. Nothing goes dark.
My business isn't fully set up yet. Is it too early?
The best time to get a business number is before it goes on anything. You don't need a website, an office, or employees, just a business name. The number you pick now is the one your business grows around.
What happens when I hire my first employee?
Add an extension, like Sales or Support, and invite them. Calls can ring both of you at once or one at a time, and they can see business texts and voicemails. Your number never changes.
Am I locked into a contract?
No. AnyPhone is month-to-month. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime. A new business shouldn't have to predict its next year to get a phone number.
