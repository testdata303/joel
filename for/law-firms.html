What you get

Everything a small law firm needs from a phone.

Business number

Get a new local or toll-free number, or bring the one clients already have. It becomes the number on your site, cards, and directory listings instead of a personal cell.

ExamplePut the firm line on the website and every listing so clients reach the firm, not an attorney's private line.

Simple call routing

Set up a short menu and point each option at the right person. New intake, current clients, and billing can reach different people without a run-around.

ExamplePress 1 for a new matter, press 2 for a current client, press 3 for billing.

Shared text messages

Texts to the business number land in one shared inbox. An assistant and an attorney can both read and reply, so a client message never gets stuck on one phone.

ExampleA client texts to confirm a meeting and the assistant replies from the shared inbox.

Voicemail and transcripts

When nobody picks up, the call goes to voicemail. You get the message as a transcript and an email so an attorney can read it between meetings and respond.

ExampleRead a new client's voicemail transcript after a hearing and call back the same day.

Voicemail transcripts and follow-up alerts

When nobody answers, Numberline captures the voicemail, sends a transcript, and helps the right attorney or assistant know who needs a call back.

ExampleA missed intake call sends an alert and the assistant calls the potential client back.

Overflow and after-hours answering

Route overflow or after-hours calls to a third-party answering service you choose. Numberline stays the phone system. It does not manage matters, automate intake, check conflicts, keep a calendar, or handle e-signature.

ExampleSend overflow calls to a third-party answering service you choose, and route current clients to their attorney during office hours.

Questions

Questions law firms ask

Can we keep client calls off personal cells?
Yes. Clients call and text your Numberline business number, and you can forward calls to an attorney's cell so personal numbers stay private. Business communication stays with the firm.
Can we bring our current number?
Numberline is designed to support bringing your current business number. Join early access and we'll share porting details when setup opens.
Can new intake and current clients ring different people?
Yes. Set up a simple menu and route each option however you like. New intake can ring an assistant and attorney, and current clients can ring the attorney on the matter.
Do voicemails come with transcripts?
Yes. When someone leaves a voicemail, you get a transcript and an email, and a follow-up alert helps the right attorney or assistant return the call promptly.
Can an assistant share the text inbox?
Yes. Texts to the business number land in one shared inbox, so an assistant and attorney can both read and reply without a message getting stuck on one phone.
Is Numberline practice management software?
No. Numberline is a phone system, not law practice management software. It helps calls and texts reach the right person. It does not manage matters, run intake automation, check conflicts, keep a calendar, or handle e-signature. It works alongside the tools you already use.
Do we need new phones or equipment?
No. Calls forward to the cell phones attorneys and assistants already carry. There is nothing to buy or install. Compatible desk phones are optional later, never required.
