People decide how good your studio is before you say a word.

Someone who found you on Instagram is calling three designers this morning. If your number rings a personal cell and you are on site with a client, it goes to voicemail - and they have already moved on to the next name on their list.

The bigger problem shows up later. Once a project starts, everything about it lives on one person's phone: the texts, the photos of tile samples, the "can we push Thursday?" messages. Nobody else can see any of it. When that designer is away, the client waits.

What you get

Everything a design studio needs from a phone.

A number that sounds like a studio

Get a new local or toll-free number, or keep the one you already give out. It goes on your website, your Instagram bio, and your invoices - so nobody ever sees a personal cell.

ExampleA first-time caller reaches “Ashgrove Interiors”, not a cell phone that rings out.

Inquiries reach a designer

A short menu sends each caller to the right place. New inquiries can ring two designers at once so the first one free picks up, while a live project goes straight to the designer running it.

ExamplePress 1 and both senior designers ring together; whoever is not on site answers.

Texts and WhatsApp in one inbox

Clients text photos of fabrics, tiles, and rooms - and plenty of them prefer WhatsApp. Both land in the same shared inbox, so any designer can pick up the thread instead of it sitting unread on one phone.

ExampleA client sends a wallpaper photo on WhatsApp; whoever is at the studio replies with the quote.

Call clients as the studio

Your designers ring clients from the phone already in their pocket, but the studio number is what shows up on the client’s screen. No personal numbers handed out, and every call back reaches a line the whole team can answer.

ExampleA designer calls from her own phone on the way to a site visit; the client sees the studio number.

Voicemail you can actually read

If everyone is busy, the caller leaves a message and you get it written out as text, plus an email. Skim it between appointments instead of holding the phone to your ear on a noisy site.

ExampleRead a new inquiry between meetings and reply before the end of the day.

Add AI answering if you want it

Point any menu option at a third-party AI answering service that picks up and takes the details, captures the message, or helps with booking depending on the service you choose. Use it after hours or all day when the studio is flat out. Turn it off any time.

Questions

Questions design studios ask

Do I need a second phone?
No - and this surprises most people. Numberline is not a handset; it is your studio number living in software. Calls to it ring the phone already in your bag, and you can call clients back from that same phone with the studio number showing.
How does calling out as the studio work?
You place the call from the Numberline app on your own phone. Your client sees the studio number on their screen, not your cell. If they ring back, it reaches the studio line - so anyone on the team can answer, not just you.
Can the whole team see project messages?
Yes. Texts and WhatsApp messages to your studio number land in one shared inbox. Any designer can read the thread, see the photos a client sent, and reply. Nothing is trapped on one person’s phone.
Does WhatsApp really work with this?
Numberline is designed to support WhatsApp alongside texts, with chats landing in the same shared inbox and nothing for your clients to install or learn. Join early access and we'll share channel availability before launch.
Can I keep the number I already give out?
Numberline is designed to support bringing your current business number. Join early access and we'll share porting details when setup opens.
What if two designers should get new inquiries?
Set option 1 to ring both at the same time and the first one free answers. You can change who rings, and in what order, whenever your team changes.
What happens if nobody answers?
The caller leaves a voicemail and you get it written out as text plus an email, so you can read it on site. You can also send that option to a third-party answering service that takes the details, captures the message, or helps with booking depending on the service you choose.
Is this project management software?
No. Numberline is only your phone system - it gets calls and messages to the right person. It sits alongside whatever you already use for drawings, schedules, and invoicing.
Are we tied into a contract?
Numberline is launching soon. Join early access and we'll send details on pricing, setup, and availability before public launch. Either way, you are never charged per person.
