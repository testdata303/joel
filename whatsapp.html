Questions about WhatsApp on Numberline

How does WhatsApp work with Numberline?
Customers message your business number on WhatsApp. Conversations land in the shared inbox alongside SMS, and the team replies from the business.
Do customers need a new number?
No. WhatsApp runs on the business number you already publish. Customers message the number on your website or business card.
Where do WhatsApp messages land?
In the shared inbox, next to your texts. Assign a conversation an owner, and the whole team sees the history.
Do replies come from the business?
Yes. Replies go back over WhatsApp from the business number, and the conversation history stays with the company, not one person's phone.
Is WhatsApp required?
No. WhatsApp is optional. Turn it on when your customers use it; SMS and everything else works the same either way. Teams that live in Slack can also get new-message alerts in Slack.
Do you charge per user?
No. Users are unlimited. Add teammates, assistants, and contractors without another seat fee.
