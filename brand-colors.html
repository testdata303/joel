Rules

How to apply it

One blue, used sparingly

Signal Blue marks the primary action. If everything on the page is blue, nothing is. One primary button per view.

Function colors are fixed

A function keeps its color everywhere it appears - site, app, diagrams, decks. AI is always pink. Voicemail is always green. No exceptions.

Solid text on soft fill

Always pair a function's solid color as text on its own soft fill. Never mix a solid from one function with a soft from another.

Two backgrounds max

White and #F6F6F7, alternating. Ink is for the occasional full-dark band only.

Never: gradients

No gradient backgrounds, no gradient text, no gradient buttons. Flat color only.

Never: blue on dark

#2D4ED8 on a dark background fails contrast. Switch to #5B79FF.
