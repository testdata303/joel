The Numberline Way

We only work for small businesses.

Every phone company in this industry starts with small businesses and leaves them the moment bigger money calls. We built Numberline so that never happens to you.

The team

Built by founders who have served hundreds of thousands of small businesses.

We know what actually matters when resources are limited, because we have spent our careers on this side of the table.

Why we exist

We work for the people who start something.

Nobody plans to start a small business at the kitchen table. It happens because a layoff landed, because the job disappeared, because working for someone else stopped making sense, or because an idea would not leave you alone.

Then comes the first phone call. And a personal cell number with a voicemail that says "hey, it's me" is suddenly the front door of a real business.

We started Numberline for that moment. For the plumber with two trucks. For the ten-person startup with contractors on three continents. For the person who got laid off on a Friday and registered an LLC on Monday. Different businesses, same act: someone put their name on something and is trying to make it work.

That act is who we work for.

Our promise

We will never outgrow you.

Not at a million customers. Not after a funding round. We do not want enterprise deals. We are not building toward bigger customers. You are not our starting point. You are the goal. The product you sign up for as a two-person shop is the product and the support you keep for as long as you run your business.

Our core values
Small businesses, forever

We build for small businesses, from the one-person shop to the twenty-person team, and we are never moving upstream. No enterprise sales team. No special treatment for bigger money. No graduating past the customers who built us. Every customer gets the same product and the same care, no matter what they pay us.

Be generous

When a decision is close, we choose the option that gives more than it takes. To our customers, to each other, to the people around us. If we are debating whether to be generous, we already know the answer.

Figure it out

Nobody here says "that's not my job" or "I don't know." If you hit a problem, you work it until you solve it. You ask for help, you try things, you learn what you need to learn. What you don't do is stop, wait for someone else, or explain why it can't be done.

Simplify everything

We make things simple because simple works better. Simple products get used. Simple processes move fast. Simple decisions actually get made. Complexity slows people down and hides problems, so we cut it wherever we find it. We are not simple for the look of it. We are simple because it is the fastest way to run a business.

Efficiency over headcount

We operate the way our customers run their businesses: a small team using the best tools in the world. Before we hire someone, we ask if a tool can do the job. Before we put more people on a problem, we give more tools to the person who owns it. Today that tool is AI. Tomorrow it may be something else, and we will be early to that too. Staying small is not a budget constraint. It is how we stay fast, and it is how we stay honest about what our customers can do.

Where we're going

A million answered.

Our goal is one million small businesses answering on Numberline, without ever signing a single enterprise deal.

Most companies in this industry believe that is impossible. They believe you have to move upmarket to survive, that small customers are a phase, that the math only works with big contracts. We believe the opposite, and we intend to prove it one answered call at a time.

When your business picks up on Numberline, you are one of the million. That counts for something here.

What we will never do
  • We will never charge per user. NO PER-USER PRICING is not a promotion. It is policy.
  • We will never make you talk to sales to see a price.
  • We will never treat the $20 customer worse than the $149 customer.
  • We will never make you learn telecom to run your phones.
  • We will never leave small businesses for bigger ones. That is the entire point of this company.
